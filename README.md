# The benchmark tool

For charities and NGOs that run a benchmark by hand: a ranking, scorecard or index that grades companies, countries or schemes. You give it your questions, your scoring rules and your documents. It finds the evidence, answers each question with a quote and a page number, scores it, and asks you about the rules it was unsure of. You check and decide everything in one page in your browser.

## Get started

1. Get a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max) and install the Claude desktop app from claude.com/download. The free plan doesn't include the Code tab.
2. Open the Claude app and click the **Code** tab. If it asks for a folder, pick any one, for example Documents.
3. Copy this prompt and paste it in:

```
Set up the benchmark tool on this Mac for me. Download https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benjyb1/benchmark-automation-handbook/main/INSTALL.md, read all of it, and follow it step by step. I'm not technical, so explain each step in plain words and run everything yourself.
```

4. Answer its questions. It asks before installing anything. When it wants to run a step, click **Allow**. After about ten minutes, your benchmark's page opens in your browser.

From then on everything happens in that page, and its Claude button (bottom right) walks you through the rest, starting with whether your benchmark suits the tool. To come back another day, double-click **Open <your benchmark>** on your Desktop.

It only works on a Mac for now. On Windows? Email benjaminbream@gmail.com.

## What it works from

Public documents (company reports, policies, websites) and any documents you give it. It cannot log into portals, members' areas or paid databases. Download what it needs from those and add it in the page.

## Your data

Everything stays on your Mac, in the folder you choose. Claude runs on your own Claude plan, under your plan's terms: no API key, no account with us, and no data sent to us. Use **Download a copy** in the page to save or share your results.

## What it costs

Runs use your Claude plan's allowance. A trial on a few rows gives an estimate for the full run before anything large starts, and nothing large starts without your go-ahead.
