# The benchmark tool

For charities and NGOs that run a benchmark by hand: a ranking, scorecard or index that grades companies, countries or schemes. You give it your questions, your scoring rules and your documents. It finds the evidence, answers each question with a quote and a page number, scores it, and asks you about the rules it was unsure of. You check and decide everything in one page in your browser.

## Get started

You need a Mac, the Claude desktop app, and a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise).

Open the Claude app, go to the **Code** tab, and paste this:

```
Set up the benchmark tool for me: read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benjyb1/benchmark-automation-handbook/main/INSTALL.md and follow it.
```

Claude explains each step and asks before installing anything. When it is done, your benchmark's page opens in your browser, and its Claude button (bottom right) walks you through the rest, starting with whether your benchmark suits the tool. To come back another day, double-click **Open <your benchmark>** on your Desktop.

Using another AI, or a Windows PC? Email benjaminbream@gmail.com.

## What it works from

Public documents (company reports, policies, websites) and any documents you give it. It cannot log into portals, members' areas or paid databases. Download what it needs from those and add it in the page.

## Your data

Everything stays on your Mac, in the folder you choose. Claude runs on your own Claude plan, under your plan's terms: no API key, no account with us, and no data sent to us. Use **Download a copy** in the page to save or share your results.

## What it costs

Runs use your Claude plan's allowance. A trial on a few rows gives an estimate for the full run before anything large starts, and nothing large starts without your go-ahead.
